## What Went Wrong / Mistakes Made

### Mistake 1: PTOC community identity assumed without verification

**What happened:** Prior work (multiple sessions before 2026-05-17) embedded "PTOC" as the primary acquisition channel — described as a "10,000-member physician Facebook group." This claim was carried through the pitch, UVP, and business model documents without any verification. Forum research in this session confirmed that PTOC = "Physicians Trained Outside of Canada" — a regulatory term, not a community.

**Impact:** The acquisition strategy in every prior document is built on a non-existent channel. The mentor session pitch would have referenced a community that does not exist in the way described.

**Root cause:** No verification step was applied to the community claim when it first appeared in the business-idea-validation-and-scoping job. Community names and their nature should be verified with a web search before they appear in any strategy document.

**Fix applied:** Part 0 of the pitch package documents the correction. Acquisition channel rebuild in Part 8 replaces PTOC with verified communities (APTA PPS, Doximity, Sermo, r/medicine, WSMA).

---

### Mistake 2: "Zero direct competitors" overclaimed without competitive research

**What happened:** Prior work stated "no direct competitors in the pre-launch physician space" — a claim that appeared in the pitch, UVP, and startup critique. Forum research in this session found five established consulting firms serving this exact market (Physicians Thrive, Independent Practice Partners, TruMediq, Cornerstone Healthcare Consulting, Physician Practice Specialists).

**Impact:** Any mentor or investor with healthcare industry knowledge would immediately challenge a "zero competitor" claim. The claim reduces credibility precisely when credibility matters most.

**Root cause:** The competitive analysis in prior sessions checked SaaS practice management platforms (Tebra, athenahealth) but did not check the unproductized consulting space. The correct claim is "no productized competitor" — which is accurate and defensible.

**Fix applied:** Part 3 of the pitch package documents the corrected competitive landscape with the "productized vs. unproductized" reframe.
