# Marketing

**Internal go-to-market brain.** Strategy, research, and the prep you take *into* customer and
client conversations. Nothing here is publicly hosted — the moment an asset ships to a URL for
credibility, partnership, or activation, it moves to [`../website/shareable-materials/`](../website/shareable-materials/).

## Folders

| Folder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `strategy/` | Business strategy, positioning, go-to-market plans, revenue model, pitch narrative |
| `research/` | Market research, industry/product research, TAM/SAM/SOM work |
| `competitive-analysis/` | Competitor teardowns, SWOT, competitive positioning |
| `product-comparisons/` | Feature/price comparisons against existing products (Carepatron, etc.) |
| `customer-research/` | Discovery interviews, customer insights, recruitment/discovery trackers |
| `customer-discussion-materials/` | Decks, one-pagers, talking points you bring *into* a live conversation (not yet public) |
| `emails/` | Outreach templates, sequences, sent-email records |
| `fraim-jobs/` | Notes/outputs from FRAIM marketing, strategy, product-research, customer-research, and GTM jobs (internal only — nothing customer- or partner-facing) |
