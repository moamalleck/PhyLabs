# Revenue Interview — Per-Prospect Scripts (5 B2C concierge targets)
**Date:** 2026-06-20 · **Companion to:** `revenue-interview-pricing-guide-2026-06-20.md`
**Model under test:** Concierge Hybrid (locked) — $1,000 PoC → Transition Program → $599/mo.
**The foil:** flat program fee **vs.** outcome-based (% of recovered revenue).

---

## Rules (read before every call)
- **Don't pitch until the close.** Steps 1–3 are pure Mom Test — their world, not our product.
- **Past behavior > hypotheticals.** "What did you pay last time?" beats "what would you pay?"
- **Say the price once, then stop talking.** Write their *first unprompted reaction verbatim* — highest-signal data.
- **Anchor the offer to *their* numbers**, not ours.

## The shared flow (every call)
1. **Current solution** — how they handle the problem today
2. **Time** — hours/week (theirs + staff)
3. **Money** — what they actually spend (software, billing %, accountant, franchise, lost revenue)
4. **Model preference (the foil)** — flat fee vs. % of recovered revenue, and *why*
5. **Price points** — Founding $8,000 (Founder-Guided) vs. Standard $22,000 (Full Done-For-You); then the outcome alt (15% of verified Year-1 recovery)
6. **Close** — the $1,000 money-back PoC

> Each script below customizes the opening + the anchors. Steps 4–6 share language; capture lines feed the pricing change-log in the companion guide.

## 1 · Saf Malleck, MD — Ortho, Ontario *(post-PoC debrief — already paid; ⚠️ family)*

**Opening:** "You're my first PoC, so I want the brutally honest version — not the supportive-brother version. Pretend you'd never met me. Walk me through whether this was worth paying for."

- **Q1 Current:** "Before the PoC, how were you finding revenue leaks — or weren't you?"
- **Q2 Time:** "How many hours a week were you (and Rob) spending on the financial side?"
- **Q3 Money:** "You said the tax was 'nauseating' and you'd have paid $10K for incorporation guidance. What did the mistakes actually cost you?"
- **Q4 Foil:** "If the program had been priced as a share of what we recovered instead of a flat fee — which would you have trusted more, and why?"
- **Q5 Price:** "Founding clinics get the guided program at **$8,000**; the full done-for-you is **$22,000**. Knowing what you now know — where does that land for you?" *(stop)*
- **Q6 Close:** "Would you convert to the **$599/mo** founder rate, locked for life?"
- **Capture:** verbatim reaction · flat vs. outcome · $8K/$22K read · $599/mo yes/no · ⚠️ weight as *directional* (family, until a non-family card is charged).

## 2 · Matthew Tucci, MD — Solo Ortho, Oshawa *(arm's-length — priority)*

**Opening:** "Saf told me you broke from the Oshawa group and built your own thing. That's exactly the person I'm trying to learn from — no pitch, just your experience."

- **Q1 Current:** "When you went solo, how did you figure out the financial/billing setup? Who did you lean on?"
- **Q2 Time:** "How much of your week goes to the business side now vs. clinical?"
- **Q3 Money:** "What do you spend today — billing service, accountant, software? And what did going solo cost you in mistakes you didn't see coming?"
- **Q4 Foil:** "Two ways people pay for help like this — a fixed program fee, or a cut of the revenue we actually recover for you. Which sits better with you, and why?"
- **Q5 Price:** "**$8,000** for the founder-guided program, or **$22,000** for full done-for-you where we run the setup. First reaction?" *(stop)*
- **Q6 Close:** "Here's the way in: a **$1,000** session on your real numbers — if we don't find $20K you're not collecting, it's free. Worth doing?"
- **Capture:** verbatim · flat vs. outcome · price ceiling between $8K–$22K · PoC committed? · card charged?

## 3 · Linda Xin, MD — Rejuvenation Derm, Oakville *(arm's-length — the done-for-you anchor; priority)*

**Opening:** "Saf mentioned you're paying around 30% overhead to a franchise. I won't assume I know your situation — but that's exactly the kind of number I help owners stress-test."

- **Q1 Current:** "What does the franchise actually do for that 30% — and what do you still end up handling yourself?"
- **Q2 Time:** "Even with the franchise, how much admin lands on you each week?"
- **Q3 Money:** "30% of a derm practice is a big number. Roughly what does that come to a year — and what would it look like to run without them?"
- **Q4 Foil:** "Would you rather pay a fixed fee for done-for-you setup, or a share of the revenue we recover? Given the franchise takes a flat 30%, I'm curious which model you actually trust more."
- **Q5 Price:** "Full done-for-you — entity, credentialing, billing, the works — is **$22,000** one-time. Against one year of 30% overhead, how does that strike you?" *(stop)* *(If hesitant: the founder-guided version is **$8,000**.)*
- **Q6 Close:** "Start with a **$1,000** session on your numbers — $20K found or it's free. Worth it?"
- **Capture:** verbatim · does $22K read as cheap vs. franchise? · flat vs. outcome · $600/mo derm sub reaction · PoC committed?

## 5 · Dr. Phan/Vu — Med Finance, Toronto *(finance-literate; possible channel)*
