## Interview script (run in this order — pricing comes LAST)

> **Per-prospect tailored scripts:** `revenue-interview-scripts-2026-06-20.md` — verbatim openings + the outcome-based foil for all 5 targets. The generic flow below is the backbone those scripts customize.

1. **Current solution:** "How are you solving this today?" *(let them describe the workaround)*
2. **Time:** "How much of your week goes to this — and your staff's week?"
3. **Money:** "What do you spend on it today — software, billing service %, accountant, franchise/MSO, lost revenue?"
4. **Model preference:** "We're weighing two ways to price this — (a) a one-time guided setup plus a low monthly, or (b) a pure monthly subscription. Which fits how you'd want to buy, and why?"
5. **Price points (only for models they accept):** present Price Point A, then B, each with its service scope. Capture the reaction, the anchor they compare to, and the ceiling.
6. **Close:** offer the $1,000 money-back PoC.

## Pricing-model change log (how feedback moves our pricing)

**Decision rules to apply as feedback lands:**
- If **≥3/5 B2C balk at $22K but accept $8K** → $1M-in-18-mo is unreachable on concierge alone at 40 customers; response = raise the volume assumption, push more customers onto $599/mo recurring, or add an outcome-based success-fee variant.
- If **B2C prefers one-time-heavy concierge over recurring** → confirm concierge as the lead motion; treat SaaS as the post-launch upsell.
- If **B2B prefers per-provider ($729) over the platform fee** → re-express the $1M-target as $729 × seat-count, not a flat $4,750 platform fee.
- If **customers repeatedly say "I'd pay based on what you recover"** → spin up a third test: % of recovered revenue (outcome-based), matching EliseAI/Canvas/athena — the best-deployed models in our competitor set.
