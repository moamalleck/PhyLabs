Shaun is open to continued engagement and willing to refer junior physicians considering practice setup decisions.

**[20:20 – 23:30] Signal 4 — EHR fragmentation: systems don't talk**
Unprompted detail on the broken EHR landscape in Alberta.

> "You end up bouncing back and forth between the two systems — and they don't. They don't really integrate at all."

> "The closest integration I get is a right-click to copy the patient's healthcare record from our EHR over to Epic — so I can pull up the patient's chart in Epic without going through a whole bunch of extra steps. But it's still basically working in tandem."

Background context: Epic/ConnectCare was originally intended to extend to private Alberta clinics, but Telus (second-largest provincial EMR) lobbied government to block the access for private practices. So private clinics use HealthQuest (legacy) while hospital work is on Epic, creating a permanent bifurcation.

**Trigger:** Ash asked about currently connected systems.
**Action:** Shaun explained the two-system workaround in detail and the political/lobby backstory.
**Outcome:** Confirms fragmented infrastructure is a real systemic issue — not just his personal choice. Validates the market need for integration/consolidation.
