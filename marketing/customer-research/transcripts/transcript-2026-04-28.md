## Items Learned

1. Ash's current product concept conflates three distinct problems at three different points in a doctor's career: clinic setup/patient intake (upstream), patient engagement and scheduling (midstream), and revenue cycle management/expansion (downstream). David identified these as potentially separate businesses or product lines.
2. Ash confirmed his focus is on the **upstream problem**: helping recently graduated residents who have decided to start their own clinic but lack awareness of what that entails (patient pipeline, marketing ROI, cost estimates).
3. The target customer moment spans a wide window, up to one year before residency completion through one year after, making it a "slow burn" rather than a single transaction trigger.
4. Ash's target specialties are **dermatology and physiotherapy**, chosen for recurring patient flow and sufficient margins to support willingness to pay.
5. Ash has two early contacts in the target stage: a graduated fellow looking for a clinic, and a resident choosing between hospital employment and starting a clinic. Neither is in the target specialty yet.
6. The core pain Ash is solving upstream is **asymmetric information**: residents are too consumed by medicine to know the steps required, and hospitals/clinic groups control the supply of available positions.
7. David confirmed that clinic business operations closely parallel gym business operations in terms of initial patient/client acquisition dynamics, referencing Alex Hormozi's Gym Launch.
8. Ash acknowledged he has **no clear signal of willingness to pay** and does not yet know at what exact moment a doctor would open their wallet.
9. David observed that residents have very limited time and energy, and early-career doctors carry significant medical school debt. Both are constraints on the monetization model.
10. David confirmed he sees "something here" in the concept and offered to stay in touch as the startup progresses.

## Other Important Notes

1. **David's central coaching theme**: Stop describing the full platform vision. Find the single moment in time when a doctor says "This is hard, I wish it was easier" and would open their wallet. That trigger is what will clarify how to talk about the problem and what to build.
2. **Discovery is not sales**: David explicitly reframed Ash's outreach goal. These early conversations are pure discovery (learning the doctor's world), not sales. Ash should not be thinking about pitching or closing at this stage.
3. **Business model sketch David proposed**: Help a doctor set up a clinic for less than the prevailing market cost (e.g., $100K market rate, position at $60K through specialization) and capture the delta as margin. This requires Ash to know the full ~50-step setup journey in detail before productizing.
4. **Monetization risk flagged**: Early-career doctors have high medical debt and limited resources. Business model validation for the upstream segment needs to account for limited willingness and ability to pay at that life stage.
5. **Narrowing will unlock everything**: Choosing a specific state and specialty combination will shrink the addressable audience enough that Ash can find 10 real people to talk to, which is where actual discovery begins.
