# PhysicianLabs — Executive Summary
**Pre-seed · June 2026 · Confidential**
*Put doctors back in the driver's seat of their own financial future.*

**What we do:** PhysicianLabs helps newly independent doctors find the money they're losing in year one — most lose $200,000 before anyone tells them why. We're the AI-native financial operating layer for independent practices: every EHR and billing tool is a rear-view mirror; we're the windshield.

---

**The problem.** Doctors train for over a decade to practice medicine and zero hours to run a business. When they go independent, they hit the same wall:
- **The complexity buries them** — entity, tax, and credentialing mistakes quietly cost $200K–$400K, usually found 18 months too late.
- **They fly blind** — no real-time view of their own P&L or operations.
- **They're squeezed** — payers reimburse an independent ~$115 for the visit a hospital gets ~$290 for. Independent practice has fallen from 60% to 42% of physicians in a decade — and the doctors choosing it now do so with conviction, with no one building for them.

**The solution.** A wedge that earns revenue today, expanding into a platform that owns the layer.
- **Wedge — First-Year Financial Clarity:** a $1,000 money-back Proof-of-Concept ("we find $20K you're not collecting, or it's free"), laddering to a done-for-you transition. Selling now; no software build required; every engagement writes the platform spec.
- **Platform — AI-native financial OS:** real-time P&L and ROI on every decision, revenue-leak / denial / credentialing intelligence, sitting on top of the existing EHR with **zero migration**.

**Market.** US revenue cycle management is a **$65B** market growing to ~$196B by 2035. Our serviceable market is ~100,000 independent practices at ~$12K blended ARPU = **~$1.2B SAM**. Entry wedge (first-year practices): **$23–46M/yr**.

**Why we win.** No one else delivers forward-looking financial intelligence, on top of the tools doctors already run, for the just-launched clinic. Incumbents (Tebra, athenahealth, Jane) are post-launch systems of record that require migration — their revenue model can't reach the first year, and their installed base is their innovator's dilemma.

**Traction (stated honestly).** 7 discovery sessions across 5 physicians in 2 countries and 5 specialties; willingness-to-pay validated at $1,000 prospective / $10,000 retrospective; first paid PoC commitment secured (delivery in progress). **Next milestone: first arm's-length, card-charged PoC within 30 days** (named warm pipeline in hand). Working prototypes built from real interviews; platform foundation live.

**Business model.** $1,000 money-back PoC → $599/mo founder rate → $500–799/mo platform subscription; one-time transition programs $750–$20K. >70% blended gross margin; program fee covers CAC inside month one.

**Financials & the ask.** Bottoms-up to **$35M ARR by year 5** (3% of SAM) — the conservative floor. The path to **$100M** is land-and-expand: same clinics, rising take as we add RCM and payments, lifting ARPU from ~$12K toward ~$25K (4% of our SAM; <1% of the $65B RCM market). **Raising $2.5M pre-seed** to reach 20 paying clinics, ~$200K revenue, and platform v1 — the proof that prices a $10M seed. Use of funds: 45% product, 25% GTM, 15% clinical/compliance, 15% ops.

**Team.** Ash Malleck, Founder & CEO — 5 years McKinsey, 11 years product at Amazon/AWS and EA, three generations of physicians in the family. Supported by an advisory bench of RCM operators, specialist accountants/attorneys, and physicians across five specialties; founding engineer and clinical-ops lead hired with this round.
