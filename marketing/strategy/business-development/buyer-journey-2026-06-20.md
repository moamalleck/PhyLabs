# Buyer Journey — Steps to Revenue (Marcus archetype)
**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Archetype:** Marcus Chen — hospital-to-independent / concierge physician, **just-launched (0–12 months)**
**Grounded in:** `one-customer-archetype-2026-05-11.md`, `icp-decision-2026-05-31.md`, `outreach-tracker.md`, the locked revenue model (`project-one-revenue-model-jun2026`)
**Revenue model:** Concierge Hybrid — $1,000 money-back PoC → Transition Program ($8K founding / $22K standard) → $599/mo subscription

---

## A. Discovery — how Marcus first encounters us
- A **peer who already transitioned** mentions us, or posts a specific, numbers-driven outcome ("PhysicianLabs caught $20K I wasn't collecting") in a community he reads — his single highest-trust channel.
- He sees that case study in **AAPP, DPC Frontier (dpcare.org), or r/whitecoatinvestor**; or in **Doximity** career-transition threads.
- A **warm cluster** (Pro Alliance / WWMG physicians splitting off) surfaces us via referral.
- Founder **direct outreach referencing something specific he said** — never cold.
- Door-opener hook: *"We find $20K your clinic is already earning but not collecting — or you pay nothing."*

## B. Research — before he's interested in trying
- He clicks through to the **interactive PoC walkthrough** (`app.physicianlab.com` demo) to confirm it's a real product.
- He **validates social proof** — searches the community for 2–3 more specific peer mentions; one concrete peer data point outweighs ten articles.
- He **tests physician-specificity** — S-corp / Form 2553 timing, corporate-practice-of-medicine law, payer credentialing, concierge membership pricing. Generic = instant no.
- He runs the **recovery estimator** (`estimate.html`) with his own numbers and sees a directional figure.
- He reads the **pricing page** and checks the price is *obviously* less than the cost of getting it wrong.

## C. Conversion moment — before he decides to buy
- **Discovery call** (Mom Test, no pitch): confirm pain, qualify to the just-launched ICP, apply the **guarantee min-threshold gate**.
- The **90-minute Revenue-Leak Audit** (run-sheet): surfaces ≥$20K traced to 4 fixable sources + Health Score + 90-day roadmap.
- The **branded one-pager** makes the number credible — every dollar tied to a source.
- The **money-back guarantee** removes the risk ("$20K or it's free").
- The **close**: commits to the **$1,000 PoC** (conversion moment), then the upsell ladder to the **Transition Program ($8K founding / $22K standard)** and **$599/mo**.

## D. Wallet moment — capturing payment into the business bank account

> 🚧 **BLOCKER (must resolve before Step D for any customer, and before any interview converts):** a connected **Stripe (or equivalent) + PhysicianLabs business bank account** must exist so the PoC can be charged and funds can settle. No payment rails = no card-charged PoC = the MVT's hard-validation gate (Tucci / Xin / Cowan) cannot close. Tracked as **Task #2**; maps to FRAIM `business-banking-setup`. Confirm current status first — may be partially set up.

- Send a **payment link / invoice** (Stripe or equivalent) for the $1,000 PoC — card or ACH.
- Marcus pays (pre-entity he may pay personally; post-entity via his new business account); **money-back terms attached** (refund window/condition).
- On program close, capture the **$8,000 / $22,000** fee (one-time, or two installments per the concierge tier).
- Stand up the **recurring $599/mo** subscription — card on file, auto-billing.
- Processor **payout settles to PhysicianLabs' business bank account** (net of fees) on its payout schedule; reconcile and issue receipts.
- *Prerequisite:* a connected **Stripe + business bank account** must exist first (FRAIM `business-banking-setup` job). Founder executes all money movement.

## E. Sales-cycle considerations
- **Length:** **Short** once social proof + a quantified PoC exist — days to ~2 weeks; the money-back PoC compresses it. The $8K–$22K program upsell stretches to **1–2 weeks** (higher consideration).
- **Who else is involved:** **B2C, sole decision-maker** — Marcus is buyer, user, beneficiary, and approver in one. No procurement/committee. *Influence vectors (not approvers):* spouse (household call at higher tiers) and the trusted peer who transitioned. *(Parked IPA path adds a group admin — Year 2.)*
- **Renewals:** **Yes** — the $599/mo subscription is a recurring monthly SaaS renewal (founder rate locked for life; churn-risk low per ICP). The program fee is one-time.
- **Trial:** The **$1,000 money-back PoC is a paid, risk-reversed trial** (guarantee = de-facto free if it fails). The **90-day free Design Partner** track is the explicit free trial for the first 5. No open-ended free SaaS trial.
