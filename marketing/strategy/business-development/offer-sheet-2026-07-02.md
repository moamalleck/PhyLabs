### Open judgment call (good pressure-test question for Ben)
External leak dollars ($150K–$400K/yr) are for *multi-specialty groups*. A **solo** independent practice is smaller, so the guarantee is anchored to the **$20K** floor — defensible for a single physician collecting ~$400K/yr at a 5% leak. **Is $20K the right guarantee floor, or should it flex to a % of the doctor's own collections?**
