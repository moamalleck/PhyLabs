Elephants in the Room
No paying customers yet.
The 30-day milestone is one paid engagement closing this month. Three warm physician referrals from my personal network are already in motion.
You are not a doctor. Why would a physician trust you?
McKinsey advisory plus Amazon PM is exactly the background this product requires. The physician needs a business framework and a specialist referral network, not a medical degree. Licensed accountants and attorneys deliver the advice. I sequence and introduce.
This sounds like consulting, not a scalable startup.
Three-stage roadmap: concierge to validate, SaaS to scale, marketplace for network effects. The concierge is the product specification phase, not the ceiling.
Doctors are notoriously hard to sell to.
The pre-launch moment is the one exception. No administrator, no procurement committee, no institutional budget approval. The physician is the sole buyer and the most motivated buyer of their career.
What stops a large incumbent from copying this?
The moat is not the checklist. It is the vetted specialist network and the community trust inside PTOC. Both take 18 to 24 months to build. A competitor can copy a feature list. They cannot copy 50 physician relationships built engagement by engagement.
