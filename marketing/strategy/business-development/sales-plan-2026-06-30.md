## The 90-Day Target (end of September 2026)

By the end of the next three months we aim to have **6 paid Proof-of-Concept (PoC) customers with cards actually charged** — at least **two of them non-family** — converting into **3 signed $8,000 founding-cohort programs** and **3 live $599/mo founding subscriptions**. That puts roughly **~$30K of cash collected** on the board over the quarter — about **3× the model's conservative base case** (1 program / 2 PoCs / $9.8K) — while pulling the first program forward from Month 3 into Month 1 and staying inside solo-founder delivery capacity (~5 concurrent engagements).

| Metric | Model base case (Mo 3) | **90-day aggressive target** |
|---|---|---|
| PoC cards charged | 2 | **6** (≥2 non-family) |
| Founding programs signed ($8K) | 1 | **3** |
| Founding subscriptions live ($599/mo) | ~1 | **3** |
| Cash collected (cumulative) | ~$9,800 | **~$30,000** |
| First card charged | Month 3 | **Week 2 of Month 1** |

This is "aggressive" only in *timing and stacking* — every number is sourced from a named, already-warm lead. It is not a volume bet; it is a conversion bet on a pipeline that already exists.

## Working Backward From the Target

### What "product" means here (zero engineering)
The revenue model is concierge / done-for-you, so "product development" this quarter is **operational productization, not software**:
1. **The PoC audit machine** — a repeatable 90-min "we'll find $20K you're not collecting" audit: discovery script, data-pull checklist, and a 1-page **Recovery Report** deliverable template.
2. **The cash register** — Stripe payment link for the $1,000 PoC + executed legal templates (money-back PoC terms, MSA) from the $1,500 setup budget.
3. **The program delivery playbook** — $8K founding-program SOW/proposal template + a fractional delivery contractor (~$2.5K/founding program) so a signed deal doesn't bottleneck on the founder.

### Sales cycle assumptions (from discovery)
- **PoC: 14–30 days** first-touch → card charged. Warm intros compress this hard (Saf already committed).
- **PoC → Program: 65% conversion**, typically **2–4 weeks** after the audit surfaces a real dollar number.
- **Subscription** starts the month *after* program signup (per model lag).

## Top risks & the lever for each
- **Slips to first program** → deepens peak cash need below −$17.5K. *Lever:* front-load the charge (Saf in Week 1, non-family in Week 3); don't wait for Month 3.
- **Family-only validation** → traction story stays weak for investors. *Lever:* Week-3 non-family card is non-negotiable.
- **Founder delivery bottleneck** → contractor onboarded in Week 3 *before* programs sign, not after.
- **Competitor (Samir Master) building in same space** → speed of proof (case studies by Week 3) is the moat this quarter.
