## Discovery Call Structure

Use this on every first call. Do not demo. Do not pitch.

1. **"Tell me how you ended up running your own practice."** — rapport, origin story
2. **"What does the financial side of the practice look like week to week?"** — surface the pain
3. **"What's the most expensive problem you haven't solved yet?"** — close setup
4. **At the end:** Deliver the Paid PoC offer (script above)
