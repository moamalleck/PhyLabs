**Date:** 2026-05-14

**Project:** PhysicianLabs
**Status:** Draft -- pending founder review

---

## Overview

Three business models are presented below. Each is written as a standalone draft covering the opportunity, value proposition, customer segments, revenue structure, go-to-market approach, key resources, key activities, key partners, cost structure, financial profile, and risk considerations.

The models are not competing alternatives. They are a sequenced progression:

- **Model 1 (Concierge Services)** is the MVP. It requires no engineering and generates revenue in 14 to 30 days. It validates the product and builds the specialist network.
- **Model 2 (Community-Led SaaS)** productizes what Model 1 proves. It converts the concierge system into scalable software and activates the viral distribution loop.
- **Model 3 (Two-Sided Marketplace)** layers network effects on top of the physician base and specialist network built in Models 1 and 2. It is the long-term moat.

The recommended launch sequence is: **Model 1 first, Model 2 in Year 1, Model 3 in Year 2.**

# Business Model 1: Concierge Professional Services

## Tagline

> The business doula for independent physicians: a founder-led guided launch service that prevents the financial and operational mistakes that end most new clinics before they find their footing.

---

## The Opportunity

Each year, approximately 20,000 new independent clinics open in the United States. The majority are launched by physicians who have spent a decade training in clinical medicine and none learning how to operate a business. The result is predictable: 92% hit a cash crisis at months two to four, 65% open without full payer enrollment, and those who delay incorporation lose $200,000 to $400,000 in preventable taxes before a specialist accountant surfaces the mistake years later.

The current workaround is informal: physicians who succeed often have a business-savvy colleague in their group who handles the logistics. Saf Malleck, a practicing orthopedic surgeon who opened two independent clinic locations, confirmed this directly. His setup was manageable because a business-oriented colleague handled the physical and operational details. Without that colleague, he said, "the vacuum is total."

The Concierge Professional Services model is the product for every physician who does not have that colleague.

---

## Value Proposition

PhysicianLabs delivers a founder-led guided walkthrough of every financial, legal, and operational decision required to open an independent clinic -- starting with the incorporation timing and tax structure decisions that have the largest long-term financial impact. Physicians who complete the concierge engagement open their clinic with the right entity structure, full payer enrollment, adequate working capital, and a trained medical secretary in place.

The alternative is discovering each mistake after it has already cost money.

---

## Customer Segments

**Primary: Solo pre-launch physicians without a business-support network**
Physicians in the final 12 to 18 months of training or employment who are planning to go independent and have no business-savvy colleague, no family member with relevant experience, and no prior exposure to professional corporation formation, payer credentialing, or operating capital planning. This is the full-information-vacuum customer. They will pay for guidance because the cost of making the mistakes alone -- as documented in the interview evidence -- vastly exceeds the engagement fee.

Target specialties at launch: physical therapy (PT) and dermatology in WA State. Orthopedic surgery and ENT as secondary markets once the checklist structure is validated across the first two.

**Secondary: High-earner specialists willing to delegate the entire setup**
Dermatologists, surgical specialists, and other high-billing-rate physicians whose hourly value of clinical time makes it economically irrational to spend 40 hours on entity formation, credentialing research, and accountant sourcing. The done-for-you tier is positioned at $5,000 to $15,000. Framing: "Your first two days of billings pays for your entire clinic setup, handled entirely by someone who has done it before."

Saf Malleck independently confirmed dermatology as the correct target for this tier, citing a direct example of a dermatologist paying 30% ongoing overhead to a franchise company for operational setup and support. A one-time $15,000 done-for-you engagement is a fraction of that cost.

**Tertiary: PE-associate physicians evaluating independence**
Physicians currently employed by private equity-owned group practices who have been offered diluted partnership economics and are actively considering going independent. Hannan Qureshi, an ENT specialist in Atlanta, described this situation directly: the founding physicians sold to PE, and new associates on a partner track receive equity that is diluted before it reaches them. These physicians are one bad partnership offer away from the independent-launch decision. The concierge tier reduces the perceived risk of going it alone.

- Post-launch monthly check-in calls for the first three months of operation

The done-for-you tier adds proactive updates as credentialing submissions are made, entity formation is completed, and billing configuration is tested. The physician receives a weekly written status update with all deadlines and next actions.

**Feasibility: HIGH.** The MVP requires a Google Sheets checklist, a Calendly link, and a Zoom account. First revenue is achievable within 30 days. The concierge model does not scale beyond 5 to 8 concurrent clients without systematizing the delivery (building the SaaS platform in Model 2) or hiring a second delivery consultant. The trigger to move to Model 2 is the moment design partner feedback has converged on the checklist structure and the first 5 to 10 engagements have documented the highest-anxiety steps.

# Business Model 2: Community-Led SaaS (Product-Led Growth)

## Tagline

> The clinical operating system for independent physician launch: a subscription platform that guides physicians from the independence decision through the first year of operation, distributed through peer testimony in the communities where physicians already spend their time.

---

## The Opportunity

The practice management software market for small independent practices is growing at 8.96% to 10.54% CAGR. It is worth $796 million in addressable revenue from physician-owned US practices alone. Every incumbent in this market (Tebra, athenahealth, SimplePractice, ChiroTouch, Jane App) enters the physician relationship after the practice is already running. None of them have a pre-launch product state. None of them guide the physician through incorporation timing, payer credentialing sequencing, or working capital planning before those decisions become irreversible.

The SaaS model occupies the pre-launch window -- the 12 to 18 months before the first patient -- and stays with the physician into their first year of operation. There are no direct competitors in this category.

The SimplePractice dislocation window (63% price increase in March 2025) has created a live acquisition opportunity in the PT market. Independent PT owners, who represent the primary beachhead, are actively evaluating alternatives at a moment when the leading incumbent has damaged the trust of its user base.

---

## Value Proposition

PhysicianLabs provides a structured, state-specific software platform that guides an independent physician through every financial, legal, and operational decision required to open and operate a clinic. The platform tracks 50 steps across entity formation, payer credentialing, working capital planning, staffing, and patient acquisition. It surfaces deadlines before they become crises and benchmarks the physician's practice metrics against specialty peers in real time.

The financial structure module is the lead element: a structured guide to incorporation timing, professional corporation tax advantages, and deductible expense categories. This is the content that no EHR vendor, no practice management software, and no financial advisor proactively delivers to a physician before the mistakes begin.

---

## Customer Segments

**Primary: Pre-launch independent physicians (PT, derm, chiro) in WA State**
Physicians in the final 12 to 18 months of training who are planning to go independent. Active in PTOC, APTA PPS, and state PT and derm associations. The beachhead is WA State at launch because regulatory content (entity formation, payer credentialing, state licensing) is jurisdiction-specific and must be accurate before expansion.

Segment score for PT: 23/25 (highest across all target specialties). PTOC community access is unmatched. SimplePractice dislocation window is live. Saf Malleck independently confirmed PT as the correct first market based on direct referral experience.

**Secondary: Established independent practices (1 to 5 physicians) experiencing tool fragmentation**
Practices already operating that are paying for four to six disconnected tools and have no integration layer. Acquired through SEO targeting practice management search intent and through PTOC peer referrals from activated pre-launch customers. This segment is the Year 2 upsell motion.

**Tertiary: SimplePractice refugees**
Independent PT owners who adopted SimplePractice and are now evaluating alternatives following the March 2025 price increase. This is a defined, addressable, and actively searching segment. Acquisition channel: targeted PTOC posting that acknowledges the price increase and positions PhysicianLabs as the alternative built for practice owners, not just practitioners.

# Business Model 3: Two-Sided Marketplace (Healthcare Professional Network)

## Tagline

> The platform that connects independent physicians to the accountants, attorneys, credentialing consultants, and medical staffing specialists who know how to help them -- so the right expert is never found by accident.

---

## The Opportunity

Every physician who has successfully navigated independent clinic setup found the right expert by accident. Saf Malleck found his specialist accountant (Rob) accidentally -- three years after incorporating, after paying personal income tax rates on practice revenue for the entire period. Hannan Qureshi's PE-owned colleagues never found the right advisor at all. The pattern is consistent across the interview dataset: the specialists who can prevent the most expensive mistakes exist in the market, but the market has no mechanism for connecting them to the physicians who need them at the right moment.

The two-sided marketplace model adds a structured expert matching layer on top of the physician product. It solves the supply-side problem that the Concierge model (Model 1) addresses through founder relationships, but at scale and without requiring the founder to be the relationship node for every connection.

The market for healthcare professional services (accountants, attorneys, consultants serving physician practices) is fragmented, expensive, and almost entirely reactive. No platform is positioned to connect pre-launch physicians to vetted specialists proactively -- before the mistakes begin.

---

## Value Proposition

**For physicians (demand side):** PhysicianLabs connects the physician to the exact specialist they need at the exact moment they need them: a physician-PC specialist accountant when the incorporation timing decision must be made, a credentialing consultant when payer enrollment begins, a medical secretary placement agency when the clinic is three months from opening. Every specialist on the platform has been vetted and reviewed by physician clients. The search that took Saf three years takes 48 hours.

**For specialists (supply side):** Healthcare accountants, attorneys, credentialing consultants, and medical staffing specialists gain access to a pre-qualified pipeline of physicians who are actively seeking their services at the moment of highest need. Listing on the platform provides qualified inbound leads without cold outreach, advertising, or referral dependence.

---

## Customer Segments

### Demand Side: Independent Physicians

The same primary, secondary, and tertiary segments as Models 1 and 2. Pre-launch physicians are the highest-value demand-side customers because they need multiple specialists simultaneously (accountant for incorporation, attorney for entity formation, credentialing consultant for payer enrollment) and they need them in a defined sequence over a 6 to 18 month window.

### Supply Side: Healthcare Specialists

| Specialist type | Service | Value of being listed |
|---|---|---|
| Physician-PC specialist accountants | Incorporation timing, professional corporation structure, income splitting, deductible expenses, retained earnings strategy | Access to the highest-intent pre-launch physician pipeline at the moment of decision |
| Healthcare compliance attorneys | PLLC/PC formation, malpractice insurance guidance, state licensing, employment contracts | Qualified inbound leads from pre-launch physicians with a defined need and timeline |
| Credentialing consultants | Payer enrollment, CAQH profile management, Medicare/Medicaid enrollment | Pre-launch physicians who need to start credentialing immediately and do not know the 90 to 120 day window |
| Medical secretary placement agencies and staffing services | EMR-trained medical office staff placement | Physicians who have cleared financial and legal steps and now face the hardest operational pain (Saf: 2,000 to 4,000 applicants, 6 months, one bad hire) |
| Healthcare-specific business coaches | Strategic advisory, growth planning, referral network development | Physicians in their first 12 months who need ongoing operational guidance beyond the launch checklist |

## Go-to-Market and Channels

**Phase 1 (Model 1 concierge engagements, Year 1):** Every specialist introduced through a concierge engagement is a candidate for the marketplace supply side. The founder vets them informally through the engagement (quality of advice, physician feedback, responsiveness). By the end of 10 concierge engagements, the platform has 5 to 10 specialists with documented track records and willing to be listed.

**Phase 2 (marketplace soft launch, Month 12 to 18):** Specialist listing pages are published for the founding cohort. Physicians who have been using the SaaS platform can browse and request introductions. Transaction fees begin on the first completed referral.

**Phase 3 (marketplace scale, Year 2+):** Specialist listing becomes a paid product. Geographic expansion (new US states, Canadian provinces) is led by specialist recruitment ahead of physician demand. Association partnerships (AICPA healthcare specialty group, state bar healthcare sections) provide supply-side acquisition channels.

---

## Customer Relationships

**With physicians:** The marketplace extends the physician relationship beyond the launch window. A physician who opens their clinic with PhysicianLabs continues to use the platform to find a billing consultant in Year 2, a second medical secretary in Year 3, and a business coach as the practice scales. Each additional specialist need is a retention event and a transaction fee.

**With specialists:** Specialist relationships are managed through a structured vetting and renewal process. Each specialist listed must have at least one verified physician review. Annual renewal requires confirming active practice, updated credentials, and at least one new physician client in the past year. The vetting process is the primary moat: it takes 12 to 18 months to build a credible vetted cohort that competitors cannot replicate by launching a directory.

The mitigation is clear: use Model 1 concierge engagements to build the specialist network before the marketplace is launched.
