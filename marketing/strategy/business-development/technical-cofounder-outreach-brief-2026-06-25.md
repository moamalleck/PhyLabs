## The opportunity
Doctors spend up to 16 years in medical training and **zero hours learning to run a business**. When they go
independent, five figures of revenue they already earned leaks away — denied claims never reworked, visits
under-coded, payers underpaying, visits never billed — and they have no visibility into any of it.

**PhysicianLabs is the windshield, not the rear-view mirror:** real-time financial intelligence for
newly-independent physicians. We find the money, recover it, then keep it visible.

## Why now / traction
- **Validated problem** across 5+ physician interviews; emotional, unprompted pain ("the tax I'm paying is nauseating").
- **A working, clickable product demo** and a manually-delivered **concierge MVP** (a $1,000 money-back revenue-leak audit — *"we find $20K you're not collecting, or it's free"*).
- **First paid commitment** in hand; design partners and a warm physician pipeline.
- **Model:** $1,000 PoC → done-for-you recovery program → **$599/mo** dashboard subscription (services-to-SaaS).
- A direct competitor is already building — **speed to a real platform is the edge.**

## What you'd build
Take the concierge service and turn it into the product. Greenfield, 0→1.
- A multi-tenant **revenue-intelligence platform** that ingests practice billing/EHR data and surfaces the
  recoverable-revenue model (the 4 leak categories) as a live dashboard.
- **Stack (open to your judgment):** TypeScript, Next.js (App Router), Node, PostgreSQL; HIPAA-aware
  architecture (no PHI leakage), auth/RBAC, EHR/clearinghouse integrations (athenahealth, DrChrono, Tebra).
- You own engineering, architecture, and the build-vs-buy calls. We are tech- and product-heavy by design.

## Who I'm looking for
- A **builder with a 0→1 track record** — you've shipped real product, full-stack, fast.
- Comfortable with **data/integrations and healthcare or fintech data sensitivity** (HIPAA a plus, not required).
- A true co-founder by the test: *we don't have a company without you, and you're not easily replaceable.*
- **Work style:** high ownership, thrives in ambiguity, no "that's not my job." Aligned on the mission to
  revitalize independent medicine.

## Who I am
Founder & CEO. Strategy at **McKinsey**, 0→1 platforms at **Amazon** and **EA**, and **three generations of
physicians** — I know this customer from both sides. I bring the GTM, customer access, capital strategy, and
domain credibility; I need the technical other half.

## The deal
- **Co-founder-level equity** — indicative **15–40%**, negotiable on timing, full-time start, cash needs, and
  what you build in the trial. Standard **4-year vest, 1-year cliff.**
- **Raising** a pre-seed/seed round; concierge revenue funds runway in the meantime.
- **How we start (de-risks both sides):** a **2–4 week paid trial project** on a real slice of the platform
  before we finalize equity — because the best predictor of a co-founder partnership is having built together.

## Where to source candidates

**Co-founder matching**
- **YC Co-Founder Matching** (free; highest-signal pool of builders actively seeking a co-founder).
- **Antler**, **On Deck**, **CoFoundersLab**, **Startup School** community.

**Healthtech / RCM / data engineering communities**
- **Health Tech Nerds**, **HLTH / ViVE** community channels, healthtech Slack/Discords.
- **r/healthIT**, **r/medicalbilling**, HL7/FHIR developer communities (for EHR-integration fluency).
- Ex-athenahealth / Tebra / DrChrono / Epic engineers on LinkedIn (they already know the data + the pain).

**General builder pools**
- **LinkedIn** (filter: full-stack/founding engineer, ex-startup CTO, healthtech or fintech).
- **Wellfound (AngelList Talent)** co-founder/founding-engineer listings.
- **GitHub** (active maintainers in TS/Next/Node/Postgres), **Indie Hackers**, relevant Discords.

**Warm network first** — your highest-trust path. Amazon/EA/McKinsey alumni networks, founder friends, and
anyone you've shipped with before (the "worked together first" rule). Ask for *intros*, not just candidates.

> Prioritize: someone you can run the **paid trial** with quickly, ideally a warm or semi-warm intro.
