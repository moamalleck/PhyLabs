## Decision Context (why most channels are wrong for us)

PhysicianLabs is a **high-ACV ($7K+ first year), narrow-ICP, trust-gated, relationship-driven sale** to independent practice owners (PT / chiro / derm / primary care; Pacific NW beachhead). That profile rules out broad, demand-*generation* channels (mass paid social, offline ads, viral loops) and favors **precise, demand-*capture* and trust-building channels** where one conversation can close a $7K commitment. Our #1 company priority is **paid PoC conversion**, so we optimize the channel mix for *qualified conversations that convert to a paid $1,000 diagnostic*, not raw traffic.

## Step 5: The Three Channels We Will Run (with effectiveness & cost)

### 1. Direct Sales / Targeted Outbound  — *the engine*
**Effectiveness — High and proven.** This is the only channel that has already produced commitments for us (the design-partner sprint signed partners off LinkedIn + Reddit + warm intros). For a $7K+ concierge sale, a one-to-one conversation that references a prospect's own words converts far better than any broadcast: high-intent warm referrals convert at 20–40%, and personalized outbound to our scored, pre-qualified ICP list runs well above generic cold-email norms (1–5% reply). It directly serves the #1 goal — booked, paid $1,000 PoCs — and gives us same-week feedback to sharpen the offer.
**Cost — Low cash, high founder time.** Effectively no media spend; the cost is labor plus light tooling: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (~$99/mo), a cold-email/sequencing tool (~$50–100/mo), and an email-finder (~$50/mo) — call it **~$200–250/mo all-in**. Blended CAC is dominated by founder hours, which is the right trade at this stage because each touch also doubles as customer discovery. Scales linearly (not exponentially) until we add an SDR.

### 2. Community Building  — *the trust multiplier*
**Effectiveness — High for our buyer; compounding.** Independent physicians buy on peer trust, and we have already mapped where they congregate (the 10,000+-member Physical Therapy Owners Club, r/physicaltherapy, r/chiropractic, DPC Frontier, association sections). Showing up as a useful community member — answering billing/credentialing questions before ever pitching — builds the credibility that makes the outbound ask land, and it generates inbound DMs that arrive pre-warmed. Effect is slower than outbound but durable and self-reinforcing.
**Cost — Lowest cash of any channel; high time + reputation.** Near-zero hard cost (a Reddit/LinkedIn/Facebook presence is free). The real cost is consistent founder time (a few hours/week) and the discipline to lead with value, not promotion — a single salesy misstep can burn standing in a tight community. Optional: a community-management tool later, but unnecessary now.

### 3. Content Marketing + SEO  — *the long-term inbound asset*
**Effectiveness — Slow to start, highest ceiling.** Our keyword map already shows real bottom-funnel intent ("WebPT alternative", "best billing software for independent PT clinic", "automate prior authorization") plus a deep top-of-funnel education surface (physician-burnout, prior-auth, patient-drop-off). Content compounds: a single guest post on KevinMD or Medical Economics reaches thousands of exactly-right readers, and ranking pages generate qualified inbound 24/7 without per-lead cost. It also feeds every other channel (outbound assets, community answers, email nurture).
**Cost — Low cash, slow payback.** $0 if the founder writes; ~$300–1,500/article if outsourced to a healthcare-literate writer; basic SEO tooling (Ahrefs/Semrush ~$100–230/mo). Expect a **6–12 month payback** before organic traffic is material — this is an investment channel, not a sprint channel, which is why it runs *alongside* (not instead of) outbound.

## What we are deliberately NOT doing yet (and why)
- **LinkedIn / Meta paid ads** — healthcare-professional CPCs ($7–15+) and typical B2B-SaaS demo CAC ($500–1,500+) are too expensive to justify against a narrow ICP we can reach for free via outbound and community. Revisit only after PMF, when we need volume over learning.
- **Trade shows / large offline events** — $3K–15K booth + travel per event; high intent but wrong cost structure pre-PMF.
- **Broad PR / viral / offline ads** — demand-generation channels that don't match a considered, high-ACV B2B purchase.

## Tone per channel (Phase 5 deliverable)
| Channel | Voice | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Direct outbound | Peer-to-peer founder, candid | Their specific pain, in their words → 20-min ask |
| Community | Helpful community member, no pitch | Answer first; offer only when directly relevant |
| Content / SEO | Credible insider (correct clinical/billing terms) | The buyer's problem and the policy tailwinds, then the windshield-vs-rear-view frame |

**Universal message principle (from Marketing Messaging work):** lead with the doctor's *fear/identity* before the solution; use verbatim buyer voice and clinical specifics over generic SaaS copy; close on the tagline — *"Put doctors back in the driver's seat of their own financial future."*

---

## Test plan & success metric (Step 4 → Step 5)
Run all three inner-ring channels as cheap parallel tests for ~6 weeks, judging each on **cost-per-qualified-conversation** and **conversations → paid $1,000 PoC**. Per Bullseye, once one channel clearly out-converts on *paid PoCs*, concentrate resources there and optimize to saturation; keep the other two as supporting/compounding assets.
