# PhysicianLabs — 3-Minute Pitch Script (Version 2)
**Date:** 2026-05-19
**Word count:** ~432 words
**Pace:** 140–150 words/minute with pauses = 3:00–3:10
**Framework:** Problem/Solution, personal narrative opening

---

> **Before you start:** Stand still. Take one breath. Make eye contact with someone in the room before the first word.

---

I come from a family of doctors.

My mother is an OBGYN. My brother is an orthopedic surgeon. My sister is a physician. My grandparents were doctors.

I am the only one who went into business.

> *[Pause. Let that land.]*

---

Growing up in South Africa, I watched my mother run a private practice and struggle financially — despite being exceptional at medicine. She still does not believe there is any benefit to incorporating her clinic. There is. The tax savings alone are material.

My brother opened his orthopedic practice and missed over $200,000 in tax savings because he structured it wrong, at the wrong time. He found out 18 months later. It could not be fixed.

My sister wants to go independent. She does not. She stays in a hospital she resents, burned out, because the financial risk of starting her own practice feels too high without a roadmap.

My best friend is a radiologist. He told me: "The healthcare system teaches you medicine. It teaches you nothing about money. But it still expects you to run a business."

> *[Pause.]*

That is the problem. And it is not just my family.

---

5,000 to 10,000 physicians go independent every year in the United States. Three preventable mistakes destroy most of them financially. Wrong entity structure costs $200,000 to $400,000 in tax losses. Credentialing errors cost $100,000 to $450,000 in lost revenue. And 92% of new clinics walk straight into a working capital crisis at months two to four.

The $30,000 to $60,000 consulting firms that solve this are real. They work. Physicians who engage them launch on time and on budget at 84%, versus 42% for those who go it alone. But they are expensive, ad hoc, and available only to physicians who already know the right people.

> *[Slow down for this line:]*

My mother did not know the right people. My brother did not. My sister does not.

---

PhysicianLabs is the TurboTax moment for physician practice formation. Same knowledge. Fixed curriculum. Fixed price.

Three tiers: $500 self-serve toolkit, $2,500 twelve-week guided program, $10,000 done-for-you. The new-launch advisory market is $23 to $46 million annually, with a $290 million ongoing subscription market behind it.

---

I spent five years at McKinsey structuring complex client decisions. Eleven years at Amazon and AWS scaling products globally. And my whole life watching the people I love navigate this problem without a guide.

> *[Pause. Then deliberately:]*

I am the right person to build this.

---

From this panel: introductions to physicians going independent in your networks, design partner conversations, and advisory perspective on scaling a professional services model.

> *[Hold eye contact. Close clean.]*

PhysicianLabs. The TurboTax moment for physician practice formation.

## Delivery Notes

| Moment | Note |
|---|---|
| "I am the only one who went into business." | Full stop. Pause 2 seconds. The room needs to register the contrast. |
| "That is the problem. And it is not just my family." | This is the pivot from personal to investable. Say it slowly. |
| "My mother did not know the right people. My brother did not. My sister does not." | Three short sentences. Do not rush them. This is the emotional peak. |
| "84% versus 42%" | Say both numbers clearly. This stat is the whole business case. |
| "I am the right person to build this." | This lands hardest after the family setup. Do not bury it in pace. |
| Closing line | Say it once. Stop. Do not add anything after it. |
