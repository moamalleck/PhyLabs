# PhysicianLabs — 3-Minute Pitch Structure
**Date:** 2026-05-19
**Format:** Demo Day / Startup Accelerator (3 minutes, 5 slides)
**Audience:** Startup accelerator mentors (VCs, founders, accelerator operators)
**Framing:** Startup-mentor lens — lead with price contrast, TurboTax analogy, market gap

---

## Guiding Principles for This Format

- **150 words/minute** at a controlled Demo Day pace = 450 words total
- **One idea per slide.** No slide should require explanation. If it does, revise it.
- **End every section with a number or a contrast.** Mentors track retention by data points.
- **Startup-mentor framing:** The emotional anchor is "productizing consulting," not "saving doctors." Lead with the $30K-$60K contrast. The TurboTax analogy closes the competitive section.
- **Never claim zero competitors.** The claim is: first productized competitor, not first mover.
- **Acknowledge the market headwind before the tailwind.** It disarms the objection before it is raised.

---

## Slide-by-Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Hook + Problem (45 seconds)
**Core message:** The pre-launch window destroys physician wealth. The gap between guided and unguided outcomes is documented.

**Structure:**
1. The story hook (10 sec): One physician. One preventable mistake. $280,000.
2. The systemic framing (20 sec): This is not a one-off. Three predictable mistakes. Dollar amounts.
3. The proof point (15 sec): 84% vs. 42% success rate. MGMA 2023. That gap is the business.

**Key data on slide:**
- $280,000 — cost of the incorporation timing mistake
- $200K–$400K — tax loss range across the customer base
- 84% vs. 42% — success rate with vs. without an advisor (MGMA 2023)

---

### Slide 2: Solution (35 seconds)
**Core message:** PhysicianLabs is the first fixed-curriculum, fixed-price program. TurboTax for physician practice formation.

**Structure:**
1. The category claim (10 sec): First productized pre-launch advisory program.
2. The analogy (10 sec): TurboTax vs. local accountant. Same knowledge. Fundamentally different vehicle.
3. The three tiers (15 sec): $500 / $2,500 / $10,000. Concierge first, SaaS second.

**Key data on slide:**
- $30K–$60K — existing consulting price
- $500 / $2,500 / $10,000 — PhysicianLabs tiers
- "Concierge is the product specification for the SaaS we build in Year 2"

---

### Slide 3: Market + Why Now (30 seconds)
**Core message:** 5,000–10,000 new practices/year. The market is declining in one dimension and growing in another — say both.

**Structure:**
1. The TAM (10 sec): 5K–10K new formations. $23M–$46M annual advisory TAM.
2. The headwind (8 sec): Independent ownership dropped from 60% to 42% since 2012. Acknowledge it.
3. The tailwind (12 sec): DPC market $61B, 10–15% annual growth. Physicians choosing independence today are doing it with conviction.

**Key data on slide:**
- 5,000–10,000 new practice formations/year
- TAM: $23M–$46M
- DPC market: $61.34B, 10–15% CAGR
- 9 new physician-ownership platforms launched in 2025

---

### Slide 4: Business Model + Traction (35 seconds)
**Core message:** Strong unit economics from day one. Concierge validates; SaaS scales; marketplace compounds.

**Structure:**
1. Year 1 revenue and volume (10 sec): $198K. 65 physicians. Zero paid CAC.
2. Unit economics (10 sec): LTV:CAC 23.5:1. Blended ARPU $4,600. Gross margin >80%.
3. The roadmap and traction (15 sec): No paid engagements yet — that changes in 30 days. Three-stage evolution: concierge → SaaS → marketplace.

**Key data on slide:**
- Year 1: $198K | Year 2: $600K–$760K | Year 3: $1.8M–$2.5M
- LTV:CAC 23.5:1 (organic) | Blended ARPU $4,600 | >80% gross margin
- CAC Year 1: $200 (zero paid)

---

### Slide 5: Team + Ask (35 seconds)
**Core message:** Unusual founder-market fit. Specific ask, not a generic one.

**Structure:**
1. The credentials (15 sec): McKinsey (5 years). Amazon/AWS PM (11 years). Brother: orthopedic surgeon, 2 independent clinics.
2. Why this fits (10 sec): The McKinsey model is structurally identical to a clinic launch engagement.
3. The ask (10 sec): Warm intros to pre-launch physicians. Design partner conversations. Advisory on productizing professional services at scale.

**Key data on slide:**
- McKinsey (5yr) + Amazon/AWS PM (11yr)
- Brother: orthopedic surgeon, 2 independent clinics opened
- Specific ask (not generic "looking for funding")

## What NOT to Say in 3 Minutes

- Do not mention PTOC (incorrect community; replaced with APTA PPS, Doximity, Sermo)
- Do not claim "zero competitors" — say "first productized competitor"
- Do not open with the market size — open with the story
- Do not use em-dashes in spoken delivery; they create unnatural pauses
- Do not hedge the ask — be specific about what you want from this panel
