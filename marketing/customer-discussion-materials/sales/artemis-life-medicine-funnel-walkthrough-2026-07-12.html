Neha — congratulations again on the launch. I went through your site the way a prospective patient would, and honestly it's one of the clearer direct-primary-care sites I've seen. A few small, high-leverage things stood out that could turn more visitors into booked Meet & Greets.

Three quick wins

1
Turn your analytics on — you can't see your own traffic
Google Analytics is installed but was never switched on. Right now, every marketing decision is a guess.
The fix  ~20 minutes to activate it and log each booking — so you see which channels actually bring patients.
2
Give researchers a way to stay in touch
Menopause patients often research for weeks. Anyone not ready to book today leaves with no way to reconnect.
The fix  A simple email capture recovers the slow deciders instead of losing them.
3
Put a price on the homepage
Your pricing is transparent — but it's one click in. A "from $200/mo" line lets visitors self-qualify before booking.
The fix  Fewer, better-fit Meet & Greets — protecting your scarcest resource: time.
