Why this money is hiding

You trained 10+ years to practice medicine and zero hours to run a business. In your first year independent, the money leaks quietly:

• Entity & tax structure — late or missing S-corp election and a poor salary/distribution split routinely cost $20K–$50K a year in needless tax.

• Revenue leakage — under-coded visits, denied claims left unworked, and uncollected patient balances bleed 5–10% of collections.

• The payer squeeze — payers reimburse an independent ~$115 vs ~$290 to a hospital for the same visit. If your contracts and coding aren’t optimized, you eat the gap.

Independent practice fell from 60% to 42% of physicians in a decade — mostly because no one shows them where the money goes.
