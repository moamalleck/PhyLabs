Why I'm building this

Doctors weren't trained to be alone in business. So I stopped pretending they should be.

I keep meeting brilliant physicians who trained for a decade to practice medicine and then got handed a second, unpaid job — running a business — with no playbook and no peers. The ones who make it usually had a room of people who'd been there. Most doctors never get that room.

UnuMedic is that room. A selective network where going independent, growing a young clinic, or backing one is a shared project — not a lonely gamble. You get the people, the tools, the events, and the operating software to put yourself back in the driver's seat of your own financial future.

I'm keeping the founding cohort small on purpose. If that's the kind of doctor you are — or want to become — I'd like to read your application myself.

— Ash
