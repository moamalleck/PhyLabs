## Market Reality

- 41.9% of physicians reported burnout symptoms in 2025 (down from 48.2% in 2023 — improving but critical)
- 40%+ of practices wait 2+ months for reimbursement; billing cycles average 6–8 weeks
- #1 complaint: disconnected tools creating duplicated work, data gaps, inconsistent support

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Pricing |
|------------|----------|----------|---------|
| Tebra (tebra.com) | 107K+ providers, budget-friendly | Not an integration layer; full EHR migration required | $99–$399/provider/mo |

| eClinicalWorks | Comprehensive | Complex; not built for solo/small clinics | Custom |
| SimplePractice | Clean UX; health/wellness | Lacks RCM depth | ~$29–$99/mo |

**Key gap:** No competitor positions as a Clinical Operating System for clinic-starters. Pre-launch and early-stage segment largely unserved.
