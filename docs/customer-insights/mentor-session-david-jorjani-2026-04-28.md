## Three Distinct Customer Moments David Identified

| Moment | Description | PhysicianLabs fit |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| M1 | Just graduated, choosing where to work (personal decision) | Not the target — too early, too personal |
| M2 | Decided to open a clinic, no clue how — "the 50 steps" | ✅ Primary wedge (H2) |
| M3 | Running 2–5 years, wants to grow or hire | Downstream (H3, H1) |

---

## Validated Signals

- The 3-product framing (launch / engagement / RCM) is correct — each is a separate problem at a separate point in time
- Dermatology + Physiotherapy confirmed as beachhead specialties (recurring flow, sufficient margins)
- "Business Doula" is a resonant pitch frame for the launch-phase product
- Gym Launch analogy validates patient intake pipeline as the entry wedge
- Medical school debt creates pricing friction for flat SaaS on H2 segment

---

## Invalidated / Challenged Signals

- Pre-launch physicians as the primary ICP: too early — the decision to go independent is the real trigger, which happens at various times
- "Clinical OS" as opening pitch: too abstract for cold conversations — save for later in the funnel
