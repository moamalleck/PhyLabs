Executive Summary

PhysicianLabs helps newly independent doctors find the money they're losing in year one — most lose $200,000 before anyone tells them why. We're the AI-native financial operating layer for independent practices: every EHR and billing tool is a rear-view mirror; we're the windshield.

The Problem

Doctors train 10+ years to practice medicine and zero hours to run a business. Going independent, they hit the same wall:

• Complexity buries them — entity, tax & credentialing mistakes quietly cost $200K–$400K, found 18 months too late.

• They fly blind — no real-time view of their own P&L or operations.

• They're squeezed — payers pay an independent ~$115 vs ~$290 to a hospital for the same visit. Independent practice fell 60% → 42% in a decade.

The Solution

• Wedge — First-Year Financial Clarity: a $1,000 money-back PoC ("we find $20K you're not collecting, or it's free"), laddering to a done-for-you transition. Selling now; no software build required.

• Platform — AI-native financial OS: real-time P&L & ROI on every decision; revenue-leak, denial & credentialing intelligence; sits on top of the existing EHR with zero migration.

Market

US revenue cycle management is a $65B market growing to ~$196B by 2035. ~100,000 independent practices × ~$12K blended ARPU = ~$1.2B SAM. First-year entry wedge: $23–46M/yr.

Why We Win

No one else delivers forward-looking financial intelligence, on the tools doctors already run, for the just-launched clinic. Incumbents (Tebra, athenahealth, Jane) are post-launch systems of record that require migration — they can't reach the first year, and their installed base is their innovator's dilemma.
